A few clear steps

Career Scout is a reusable workflow for ChatGPT and Codex. Start with your own information, then decide what happens with each job.

01 · Set up

Build your own profile

Choose the roles, locations, pay, schedule, and benefits that matter to you. Use a custom answer when the listed options do not fit.

02 · Review

Understand the fit

Compare openings in a compact table with pay, work arrangement, fit, and missing details. Open the original job link whenever you want.

03 · Choose

Act on each job

Use the supported conversation controls to choose Apply on my behalf, Apply later, or Archive. Opening a job link is a separate choice.

04 · Approve

Use your approved materials

Career Scout starts in Review Mode. Review the prepared application and approve the submission. Actual submission depends on available tools and employer requirements.

Start with your own information

Each candidate supplies their own résumé, profile, tracker, and permissions. The reusable plugin contains no creator résumé, candidate profile, or application history. Reusable demonstrations use entirely fictional information.

Career Scout works with the candidate’s own authorized accounts and storage. Search, documents, email, forms, tracking, and optional scheduling depend on supported tools. The plugin provides no separate server or background service.

Public access is being prepared

Blossom Heritage Technologies intends to offer Career Scout free in all available countries. Availability also depends on ChatGPT, Codex, connected services, and local job listings.
