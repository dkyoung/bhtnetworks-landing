These proposed terms describe the Career Scout workflow provided by Blossom Heritage Technologies (“BHT”).

Review draft only, with no effective date. BHT must confirm its business identity and adopt the final terms before they take effect. This draft does not create an agreement to use Career Scout.

1. The service

Career Scout is a free reusable workflow for guided job-search intake, job matching, application preparation, and applications authorized by the candidate. BHT charges no plugin access fee and offers no purchases or paid subscriptions through Career Scout.

Career Scout depends on ChatGPT or Codex and available connected services. Those services may have their own access requirements, charges, geographic restrictions, and terms. Career Scout provides no separate server or background service and does not guarantee that every employer’s application process can be completed through the available tools.

2. Your accounts and information

Use Career Scout only if you meet the eligibility and age requirements of the host platform and connected services. Use your own accounts or accounts you are authorized to manage. You are responsible for the information, files, and permissions you provide.

Provide truthful employment history, qualifications, and application answers. Do not misrepresent another person’s identity, credentials, work authorization, or consent. Do not use the workflow for spam, fraud, unauthorized access, or activity that violates applicable law or a service’s rules.

You retain your rights in your résumé and other content. These proposed terms do not grant BHT a general right to publish your information, use it in demonstrations, or use it to train models.

3. Application decisions and authorization

Career Scout starts in Review Mode. Check the job, recipient, résumé, cover letter, and application answers before authorizing a submission. An “Apply on my behalf” choice starts the application workflow; it is not evidence that an application has been submitted.

Any automatic-application permission must have an explicit scope, such as permitted jobs, approved materials and answers, limits, and an expiry. You may revoke future authorization. Revocation cannot undo an application already delivered to an employer or service.

Do not authorize an action using information you have not checked or permission you do not have. Employer eligibility decisions and application outcomes remain with the employer and relevant service.

4. Accuracy and availability

Job listings, pay, requirements, and availability can change. AI-generated matches and application drafts can contain mistakes. Verify material information and correct errors before relying on it or sending an application.

Career Scout does not promise an interview, an offer, a particular salary, a completed application, or uninterrupted availability. It is a job-search aid rather than a hiring decision-maker. Features may change as the plugin, host platform, or connected services change.

5. Privacy and third parties

The privacy review draft describes the proposed handling of support information and the website’s technical data. The final privacy notice must be adopted alongside these terms.

Each candidate uses their own profile, tracker, accounts, and permissions. Sending an approved application shares the selected information with the employer or application service. Connected services, job boards, employers, and OpenAI have their own terms and privacy practices.

Contact support with only the information needed to explain the issue. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, Social Security numbers, or unrelated personal information.

Career Scout is offered on an available-services basis, subject to the capabilities and limitations described above. Nothing in these proposed terms excludes or limits rights, remedies, or liabilities that applicable law does not permit the parties to exclude or limit.

No special arbitration requirement, class-action waiver, indemnity, chosen court, governing-law clause, or fixed monetary liability cap is proposed in this draft.

You may stop using Career Scout and manage or disconnect accounts through the relevant host or service settings. BHT may update the reusable workflow, change its public listing, or stop offering future versions or support.
