Privacy notice · publisher review draft

Your information,
your job search.

This proposed notice covers the Career Scout plugin and its dedicated overview, support, privacy, and terms pages. It does not cover BHT’s separate commercial wireless services.

This is review text, not a final policy. It has no effective date. The proposed practices below must be confirmed and implemented by BHT before this notice is published as final.

1. Who provides Career Scout

Blossom Heritage Technologies (“BHT”) provides Career Scout, a free reusable workflow for guided job-search setup, job review, application preparation, and authorized applications. The plugin has no separate BHT-operated server, candidate database, or background service.

Privacy questions can be sent to domingo@bhtnetworks.com.

Publisher check: BHT’s exact verified business identity must be checked before final publication.

2. Information used for your job search

Career Scout may work with the résumé, employment history, job preferences, application answers, job listings, and tracker information you choose to provide. It uses available tools within your authorization and your own ChatGPT, Codex, storage, and connected-service accounts.

Installing this instructions-based plugin does not automatically send your conversations, résumé, application tracker, or connected-account contents to BHT. If you email that information to BHT for support, it becomes part of your support message.

OpenAI, connected services, job boards, and employers process information according to their own terms, privacy notices, and account settings. An application you authorize may disclose your approved materials and answers to the employer or application service involved.

The reusable plugin package contains no creator résumé, private candidate profile, or application history. Reusable examples are entirely fictional. Candidate or support information must not be copied into public examples, demonstrations, or plugin updates.

3. Information BHT receives through support

If you contact the support email, BHT receives your sender address, any name displayed by your email account, your message, attachments you choose to send, and email delivery information. BHT’s support collection outside your own ChatGPT and connected accounts is limited to those voluntary requests.

Send only what is needed to explain the issue. Please exclude passwords, authentication codes, Social Security numbers, full résumés, and unrelated personal information.

Provider check: The business email provider, any forwarding or ticketing services, and any separate support archive must be confirmed before this section is finalized.

4. Website hosting and technical information

Vercel hosts the dedicated Career Scout pages. Serving and securing website requests can involve technical information such as IP addresses, requested URLs, browser or device information, timestamps, and security or diagnostic logs. Vercel describes its handling of service and website information in its privacy notice.

The dedicated Career Scout pages contain no support form, account sign-in, custom analytics script, or advertising tracker. This statement is limited to these pages; the rest of the BHT website may use different features.

Configuration check: Confirm any Vercel log drains, additional analytics, or separate exports before publishing a final description of technical-data retention. No fixed hosting-log retention period is asserted in this draft.

5. Proposed use, access, and sharing rules

The proposed rule is to use support information only to respond to the request, troubleshoot the issue, maintain service security, handle privacy requests, and meet applicable legal obligations. Access would be limited to authorized BHT personnel and service providers who need it for those purposes.

Email providers process support mail to deliver and store it. Vercel processes website requests to provide hosting. Information may also need to be disclosed when required by law or to address a security incident. Using these services can involve processing in other countries under the relevant provider arrangements.

The proposed rule excludes selling support information, advertising use, model training, and reuse in public examples. These are proposed BHT commitments, not a description of OpenAI’s or another provider’s independent practices.

6. Retention and deletion — decision pending

Proposed retention: Keep support correspondence while the request is open, then delete active support records within 90 days after resolution unless a specific legal obligation requires longer retention. The 90-day period has not been adopted.

Proposed deletion requests: Accept requests at the support email, verify control of the relevant address using only necessary information, and respond within 30 days. Delete the relevant active support records unless retention is legally required; explain any applicable exception. This process and period have not been adopted.

Provider copies: Confirm how the email service handles deleted items, synced devices, exports, backups, and legal holds. This draft does not promise immediate erasure from every backup or another provider’s systems.

Retention of your ChatGPT conversations, connected-service files, job-board records, and employer applications is controlled through those services. BHT cannot erase information from an account it does not control.

7. Your choices and requests

You choose what information to provide and which accounts or application actions to authorize. You may use the relevant provider’s settings to manage connected accounts, stored conversations, and files.

You may contact BHT about support information, including requests for access, correction, or deletion and other rights available under applicable law. The final notice will describe the confirmed request process. Mandatory legal rights and deadlines take priority over any operational target in this draft.

Career Scout users must meet the eligibility and age requirements of ChatGPT or Codex and any connected services they use. BHT’s intended audience and the required submission attestations still need confirmation; this draft does not make an unverified children’s-data declaration.

8. Questions and changes

Contact domingo@bhtnetworks.com with Career Scout privacy questions. The final notice will show an effective or updated date. Material changes to the stated practices will need a corresponding notice update.
