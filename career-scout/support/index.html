Career Scout support

How can we help?

For questions about setting up or using Career Scout, contact Blossom Heritage Technologies.

domingo@bhtnetworks.com

What to include

  • A short description of the problem or question.
  • The steps that led to it and what you expected to happen.
  • Only the information needed to explain the issue.

Please leave passwords, authentication codes, Social Security numbers, full résumés, and unrelated personal information out of support messages.

About your support message

Outside users’ own ChatGPT and connected accounts, BHT collects only the support requests users choose to send. An email shares your sender address and message content with BHT and the email providers involved. It does not grant access to your ChatGPT account or connected services.

This page has no support form, account sign-in, or added analytics. The website is hosted on Vercel.

The support-request retention period and deletion process have not been finalized. The full privacy policy and terms of use will be linked before public release.
